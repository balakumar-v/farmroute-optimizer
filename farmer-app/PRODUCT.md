# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (installable PWA, phone first)

## Users

Smallholder farmers in an FPO (farmer producer organisation), many with low literacy, using a shared or low-end Android phone, often on weak signal, outdoors in bright sun. They log in with a farmer ID and a PIN given by the FPO. They speak Telugu, Hindi or English. They want three answers: is the truck coming, when, and how much will I get.

## Product Purpose

Shows one farmer only their own card from the plan their FPO published: status (hauled, sold at the doorstep, no truck free), pickup time, truck, mandi, and an honest payout with each number tagged exact, derived or assumed. Success: a farmer understands their day in under ten seconds and can have it read aloud.

## Mode

Operate: a task-completing tool, not a showcase.

## Honesty rules

Times are planned, not live tracking. Prices tagged "assumed" are estimates, never promised quotes. Never show another farmer's data or trip-level revenue. Always show when the card was last updated; the app must work offline from the last card.

## Anti-references

Dashboards, charts, maps as decoration, dense tables, jargon (pooling, ILO, LP), gamification, loud colour, English-only text.
