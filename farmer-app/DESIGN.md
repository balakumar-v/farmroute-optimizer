# Design

Applies to every direction; each sample (A Slip, B One big answer, C Journey) picks its own palette within these rules.

## Principles
- One idea per screen; the answer comes first (status, then time, then money).
- Words and an icon together; never colour alone.
- Large and plain: body 16px minimum, key facts 28px and up, tap targets 44px minimum (primary 56px).
- Sunlight: contrast at least 4.5:1, flat light surfaces, no thin grey text.
- Calm: no gradients, no animation beyond a short fade; no emoji; one consistent stroke icon set.

## Type
Mukta (Latin and Devanagari) with Noto Sans Telugu for Telugu. Three sizes carry the layout: fact (28 to 84px, weight 800), label (16 to 18px), note (14px).

## Tags
Exact, Derived, Assumed appear as outlined pills with the word in them. Estimates are always labelled.

## Required states
Hauled, sold at the doorstep, no truck free, changed since last plan, not published yet, offline (shows last card and its time), session expired, locked out.
